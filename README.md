# WARP_Shoe_company
This project report conducted its analysis into a series of shoe production process, combining theoretical models and tools obtained from course materials with our own assumptions and thinkings, aims to maximize the net profit of WARP Shoe Company. 2. Introduction In this project we are asked to help WARP Shoe Company to gain maximum profit by constructing a LP model. At the beginning of 2006, a strong competitor of WARP went bankrupt. The market analyst in the WARP company predicted that demand for shoes on the market would double. The company aims to utilize this period by updating a profitable production plan. We will apply models and tools obtained in this operation research course to model the problem.
The whole problem is structured by a relaxed LP model. We will start by constructing the objective function and constraints to structure the model. We will be using AMPL to test and optimize feasible solutions for this model. Each variable value will be rounded up (if decimal part > 0.5) or rounded down otherwise to its closest integer, finally composing our production plan for this company. We will also give a analysis
of strength and limitation of this model as an evaluation.
